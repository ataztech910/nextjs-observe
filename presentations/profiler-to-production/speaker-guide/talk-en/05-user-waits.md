# 05 · Which wait are we measuring?

`step: user-waits` · Why this happens · **2:20 → 3:00** (0:40)

**On screen:** three waits—open, act, move. Do not read the card aloud.
**Purpose:** replay the opening scene slowly through one person’s eyes. The three waits are three moments in one visit, not three items on a list. End at the click, where things broke.

## Script

Let’s go back to the opening scene and slow it down.

Someone is looking for a cheaper product. They open the page and wait for the first thing: when can I start? Not when the header is painted, but when I can browse the products. That part is fine—two tenths of a second.

They scroll through the list and expect the scrolling to stay smooth. We won’t measure that wait today, but it’s there too.

They click “Price: low to high” and wait for the third thing: the sorted list. Not a highlighted button. The result.

And here—nothing.

*(pause)*

Three waits in half a minute. One person, one page. Each has its own start and finish. Lighthouse honestly measured the first. It knows nothing about the third.

## Transition

> To measure that third wait, naming it isn’t enough. We need to say exactly where it starts and where it ends.
